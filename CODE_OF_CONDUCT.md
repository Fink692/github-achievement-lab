# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Welcome questions, explain decisions, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information.

## Reporting

If a concern cannot be handled in the public issue tracker, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept as confidential as practical.
